The process

Here's how it works

  1. Start

    Open a ticket

    Join the Discord, go to #open-a-ticket and click Open a ticket. Please don't send DMs.

  2. Brief

    Send your references

    Your character or your Roblox skin, the type of piece, pose, mood and any deadline.

  3. Delivery

    Get your art

    You get a price based on complexity, pay with PayPal or Robux, and receive your piece.
